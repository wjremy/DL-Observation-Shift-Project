## Other candidates checked

- **Neonatal:** [PICSDB](https://physionet.org/content/picsdb/1.0.0/) is genuinely open (ODC Attribution), but has only 10 infants, ECG/respiration waveforms and bradycardia annotations—not hospital labs or a suitable outcome; **1.6 GB**.
- **Brazil FAPESP COVID-19 Data Sharing/BR:** official FAPESP sources describe an earlier release with 177,000 patients, about 9,600 outcomes and nearly 5 million tests. The [current repository and item pages](https://repositoriodatasharingfapesp.uspdigital.usp.br/) were unreachable during this check, so current access terms, direct download and file sizes are **not verified**.
- The [Mexico COVID-19 Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/5999622) is marked Open and lists a 327.3 MB file, but its relevant time-series/outcome description and licence are **not found**.
- Spain’s [Sanitas COVID data](https://landing.sanitasweb.es/data/opendatacovid/english.html) requires an assessed request; HM Hospitales’ [CDSL PhysioNet release](https://physionet.org/content/covid-data-shared-learning/1.0.0/) requires credentialing, CITI training, DUA and contributor review.
- Yan et al.’s Wuhan data are not a verified open multi-hospital download: the authors state partner-hospital data were shared under confidentiality terms ([author reply](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-020-00251-5)).

## Excellent but excluded for access

- Full [MIMIC-III](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciii/1.4/) and [MIMIC-IV](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/): PhysioNet credentialing, CITI training and DUA.
- Full [eICU-CRD](https://physionet.org/content/eicu-crd/2.0/): credentialing, training and DUA.
- [HiRID](https://physionet.org/content/hirid/1.1.1/) and [SICdb](https://sicdb.com/Documentation/SICdb_Documentation): credentialed access/DUA; HiRID also requires CITI and study review.
- [AmsterdamUMCdb](https://github.com/AmsterdamUMC/AmsterdamUMCdb): approved request, signed agreement, required research course and clinician reference.
- Chinese [Zigong infection ICU](https://physionet.org/content/icu-infection-zigong-fourth/1.1/), [Zhejiang ICU](https://physionet.org/content/zhejiang-ehr-critical-care/1.0/) and pediatric [PIC](https://physionet.org/content/picdb/1.1.0/): credentialing/DUA and training.

## Recommendation

For a one-week start, use **P19 A/B** as the clean two-hospital comparison, then add **P12 set A** split by ICU type as older BIDMC unit/era domains. Add **SepsisExp** as a third hospital’s sepsis cohort (26 MB, 296 cases), but its expert label and carry-forward preprocessing limit direct comparison of the measurement mask. These downloads total about **76 MB**. The eICU demo adds a pooled 20-hospital cohort for another 130.4 MB, but its page does not establish site-specific event counts or actual hospital identities.

**I could not verify three independent hospitals with ≥50 outcomes each and a clearly preserved test-order mask** under the no-login constraint.
